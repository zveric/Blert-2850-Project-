# Farm Hand
### Overview
- Farm hands have responsibility for the everyday running of the small holding.
- They keep track of where the animals are grazing, when they need to be moved, and the general health of the animals.

### Goals and Success Criteria
- To be able to see the live location of the animals to make sure they haven't escaped / been stolen.
- To be able to check an overview of the livestock's health.

### Pain Points
- Delayed warning of animals escaping.
- Expensive data costs
- Limited mobile reception
- Information overload

### Constraints and Non-goals
- Only needs live data and trend summaries, uninterested in historical data.
- Limited mobile reception.

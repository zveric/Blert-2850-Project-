# Police Department
### Overview
- Works for the local farmers to catch cattle burglars.

### Goals and Success Criteria
- To be able to track the live location of the animals when they have escaped / been stolen.
- To be able to catch and apprehend livestock thieves.

### Pain Points
- Delayed warning of animals escaping.
- Signal Can't differentiate which animal is which.
- Limited mobile reception.
- Information overload.

### Constraints and Non-goals
- Doesn't care about the welfare / health of the livestock
- Doesn't need to know the location of animals whilst they within the geofence

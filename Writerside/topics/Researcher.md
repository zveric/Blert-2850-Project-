# Researcher
### Overview
- Works with 100s of small holdings in South Africa.
- Studying the grazing patterns of cattle.

### Goals and Success Criteria
- Exporting raw data of cattle movement.
- Details in an easy read format (such as csv).
- View useful visualisations of data.

### Pain Points
- Delay causing greater expense.
- Limited in time.
- Incomplete data / gaps in the data.
- Complex / hard to read data structures.

### Constraints and Non-goals
- Not being able to download / easily export data.
- Doesn't need live current data of animals.

# Personas
A persona is a representation of a real-life user of the system.
Due to time and resource constraints we are unable to conduce full user studies. However, we have been able to speak with some students from Fort Hare University who have local knowledge about how smallholdings function in South Africa and the challenges that the farmers face . We have used the information we have gathered from these conversations to create personas that represent the users of our system.

## Farm Hand
### Overview
- Farm hands have responsibility for the everyday running of the small holding.
- They keep track of where the animals are grazing, when they need to be moved, and the general health of the animals.

### Goals and Sucsess Criteria
- To be able to see the live location of the animals to make sure they havent escaped / been stolen.
- To be able to check an overview of the livestocks health.

### Pain Points
- Delayed warning of animals escaping.
- Expensive data costs
- Limited mobile reception
- Information overload

### Constraints and Non-goals
- Only needs live data and trend summaries, uninterested in historical data.
- Limited mobile reception.


## Veterinarian
### Overview
- Have responsibility for the health of the animals in the small holding.
- They keep track of the animals health and their wellbeing needs.
- They dont have a day to day role on the smallholding (works at many smallholdings) and are only involved in proactive care and emergencies.
- Needs to locate animals quickly and efficiently during emergencies.
### Goals and Sucsess Criteria
- Track animals health and wellbeing needs.
- Quickly locate animals in emergencies.
- Predict animals health and wellbeing needs before they occur.

### Pain Points
- Limited mobile reception.
- Animals fall ill / die.
- Incomplete information on animals heath metrics makes clinical decisions difficult.


### Constraints and Non-goals
- Only concerned with animals health and wellbeing needs, and therefore warnings and alerts about the animals location are not important.

# Personas
A persona is a representation of a real-life user of the system.
Due to time and resource constraints we were unable to conduct full user studies. However, we were able to speak with some students from Fort Hare University who have local knowledge about how smallholdings function in South Africa and the challenges that the farmers face. We used the information we gathered from these conversations to create personas that represent the users of our system.
